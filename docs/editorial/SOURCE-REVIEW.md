# Editorial voice: source review

Reviewed **8 October 2026**. This is a voice revision of the same ten locations, not a content expansion. History remains in `history`; attributed ghost lore remains in `reports`. The shared data contract and all classifications, phenomenon tags, access fields, coordinates, official links, media records and publication fields are unchanged. Card introductions use the existing `summary` field.

The review fetched the existing operator, heritage and reference pages directly and read their relevant text. Of 32 catalog source references, 27 returned readable material on the initial pass. One temporary history-page failure succeeded on retry. Two outdated URLs were replaced with their current official counterparts, documented below. Two Visit Pendle pages could not be fetched from this workspace; its existing walking-trail PDF was readable. No failed request was treated as confirmation of its contents.

## Boundaries of this revision

- No new location, review, witness, rating, quotation, photograph or phenomenon tag.
- All historical information is retained; generic proof disclaimers are replaced by natural attribution, not removed source records.
- Every ghost-story detail remains traceable to an existing source, or to the documented official replacement for the same source.
- Existing internal verification, sensitivity and pre-launch review requirements still apply. This voice pass does not approve a public production launch or complete the wider content-review milestone.
